Terms of Service

Last updated: April 2026

1. What TogetherMindsAI is

TogetherMindsAI is an AI assistant that licensed clinicians use to support the sessions they lead — giving the clinician private suggestions, technique reminders, and risk alerts. The app itself is not a licensed mental health service, a medical service, or a substitute for the professional care a clinician provides.

The AI assistant is not a therapist, psychologist, psychiatrist, counsellor, or licensed professional. Nothing it produces is a diagnosis, treatment, or clinical advice; the clinician leading the session is responsible for all clinical decisions.

Not every account is clinical. TogetherMindsAI is also used by practitioners who are not licensed health providers — such as hypnotherapists and coaches — and by caregivers monitoring a baby or a patient. Where these terms refer to licensure certification, a clinical record, or the clinician's responsibility for clinical decisions, they apply to clinician-led sessions. A session led by a non-clinical practitioner is not health care, creates no clinical record, and no diagnostic or billing codes are produced.

2. Who can use this service

You must be 18 years of age or older to use TogetherMindsAI. By continuing past the Getting Started screen you confirm that you meet this requirement.

Before joining a session you tell us the location (U.S. state or country) you are physically in. Your clinician must then confirm they are authorized to provide services to a client located there before you are admitted. TogetherMindsAI does not verify licensure and does not vet cross-border or international authorization — whether a clinician may lawfully serve a client in a given place is the clinician's professional judgment and responsibility. Telehealth and cross-border licensing rules vary and can be complex; the clinician's certification is their attestation, not a determination by us.

If you are under 18 and need support, please speak with a trusted adult or contact a crisis line such as 988 (US, free, 24/7) or text 988, or text HOME to 741741. Outside the US: findahelpline.com or befrienders.org have directories for your country.

3. This is not emergency support

TogetherMindsAI is not an emergency service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call your local emergency services — 911 (US), 999 (UK), 000 (Australia), or 112 (EU) — or go to your nearest emergency room.

If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, please contact a crisis line:

  • 988 — Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US, call or text, 24/7)
  • 741741 — Crisis Text Line (US, text HOME)
  • 116 123 — Samaritans (UK & Ireland, free, 24/7)
  • findahelpline.com — find a line in your country
  • befrienders.org — find a line in your country
4. Your data in clinician-led sessions

When a licensed professional leads a session, the conversation is treated as part of that clinician's clinical record. We do not collect your name, email, or phone number; the session is identified by a randomly generated code.

Messages are stored encrypted and are never used to train AI models, sold, or shared. They are accessible to and downloadable by the clinician who led the session.

Such sessions are retained for up to six (6) years, consistent with clinical-record retention practices. The clinician is responsible for the record.
