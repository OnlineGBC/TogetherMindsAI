Rendered by three different outcomes — the launch itself, a filed note, and a refused note — so every branch below has to read sensibly with the patient block empty. After a write there is no fresh read from the chart, because a write is not a reason to go and re-read a patient. The note box is pre-filled by the caller and edited by the clinician. Nothing here files anything on its own: a progress note is a permanent medical record entry, and the button press is the approval. #} EHR launch — TogetherMindsAI
