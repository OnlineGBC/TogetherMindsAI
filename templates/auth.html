1. What TogetherMindsAI is

TogetherMindsAI is a baby cam. It sends live video and sound from one device, such as a phone or tablet in your baby's room, to the device of the person watching.

It is not a medical device and it does not replace a doctor, a nurse, or an adult who is watching your baby. Do not rely on it as your only way to keep your baby safe.
