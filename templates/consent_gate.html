Before you join this session

Please read this and agree to continue.

Your speech in this session is transcribed to text by an automated (AI) service, and that text becomes part of the session transcript — your clinician's confidential record. Your session is not recorded by default — only the text is kept.

Recording the audio/video is a separate, optional feature that stays off unless every participant consents, and anyone can decline or stop it at any time. If a session is recorded, the audio/video is kept for 30 days and then permanently deleted; the written transcript is kept as the clinical record for up to 6 years.

This session is also supported by a private AI co-pilot that assists your clinician; it does not replace their professional judgment. Speech and text are handled by our service providers under signed HIPAA agreements — see our Privacy Policy.

By continuing, you consent to live AI transcription as described. You can turn your own transcription off at any time from the audio controls.
