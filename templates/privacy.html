Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 16, 2026

1. About This App

TogetherMindsAI is an AI-assisted reflective support tool operated by Global Business Consulting, Inc. (GBCai4org). It is not a licensed mental health service and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

2. What We Collect
  • Session messages — the text you send during a session
  • Session ID — a randomly generated code used to identify your session
  • Display name — the name you choose within a session (need not be your real name)
  • Usage metadata — timestamps, session mode (solo/couple/group), and rate-limiting data

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials. No account is required to use the app.

3. How We Store It

All session messages are encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption. Data is stored in a Google Cloud SQL database hosted in the United States.

4. How Long We Keep It

Clinician-led sessions are clinical records, retained for up to 6 years, consistent with clinical-record retention practices. Audit logs are also retained for 6 years as required by applicable regulations.

5. Who We Share It With

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. The following third-party services process data on our behalf:

  • Google Cloud Platform — hosting and database (google.com/privacy)
  • Anthropic — AI response generation; messages are sent to Anthropic's API to generate responses (anthropic.com/privacy)
6. Age Requirement

This app is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

7. Your Rights

To request access to or deletion of a session's data, contact the clinician who led the session (the record-holder), or us, and provide the Session ID. Note that clinical records may be subject to retention obligations that limit deletion.

8. Crisis Resources

If you are in crisis, please contact a real person immediately:

  • US: call or text 988, or text HOME to 741741
  • Worldwide: findahelpline.com or befrienders.org
9. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests:
Global Business Consulting, Inc. (GBCai4org)
Email: privacy@onlinegbc.com

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.
