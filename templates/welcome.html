Welcome to TogetherMindsAI

A reflective space to talk through what's on your mind — Anonymous · Free

100% Anonymous

No signup, no email.

No signup. No email. No personal information collected. Just a 6-character session code that's yours alone — share it to invite a partner or group, or keep it private for solo reflection.

Always Free

No paywall, no subscription.

No paywall, no subscription, no upsells. Use it as much as you need, whenever you need it. Your wellbeing shouldn't depend on a payment plan.

Three ways to reflect
Solo

One-on-one with the AI.

A private one-on-one conversation with the AI. Vent, journal aloud, or work through what's on your mind at your own pace. The AI reflects back what it hears and asks gentle follow-up questions.

Couple

Real-time chat with your partner.

A shared real-time session with your partner. Both of you join the same room from any device using one session code. The AI moderates, surfaces what might have been missed, and keeps the conversation grounded.

Group

Host or join a small circle.

Host or join a small group circle — friends, peers, a support group. Everyone joins with the same session code. The AI moderates the room and reflects on what comes up across the group.
